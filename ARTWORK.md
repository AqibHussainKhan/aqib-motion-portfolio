# Artwork assets

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool for this portfolio, then resized and encoded as WebP for delivery. The original generated artwork is not needed at runtime.

- `public/images/hero-chrome.webp`: an anonymous masculine humanoid sculpture seated on a minimal tubular chair, made from dark reflective chrome with silver highlights and violet rim lighting. Violet architectural studio, lavender spotlight, floating four-point star. Portrait composition, no text or branding.
- `public/images/lime-loop.webp`: a monumental twisted loop made from black chrome and acid-lime lacquer, suspended over a muted sage ground. Editorial architectural composition, realistic reflections and soft shadows, no text or branding.
- `public/images/blue-portal.webp`: a monumental archway of concentric metallic lavender and icy-blue ribs in a cobalt landscape, luminous center, mist and a reflective floor, small silhouette for scale. Cinematic architectural composition, no text or branding.

The exact generation prompts are retained below.

A futuristic anonymous masculine humanoid sculpture seated elegantly on a minimal tubular cantilever chair, one knee raised, arms relaxed, body made from polished dark liquid chrome with bright silver reflections and violet edge lighting. A small radiant four-point chrome star floats near one hand. Stage it in a vast architectural studio with an intensely saturated electric violet floor and a dark indigo/violet wall, a crisp soft rectangular lavender spotlight behind the figure. Full body visible, centered in lower two-thirds, plenty of negative space at top. Sophisticated editorial 3D render, cinematic but clean, glossy materials, realistic soft shadows, slight fine film grain, restrained color. Palette electric purple #6528ff, lavender, black chrome, warm silver. No text, no typography, no logos, no UI, no watermark. Not a real person or portrait; unmistakably a sculptural art object.
